Why 23-7

Built on Hard Work and Straight Talking

The name 23-7 isn't a gimmick. It's a mindset. Most trades work 9-to-5 and you're lucky to get a callback before Tuesday. Robert built this business around being genuinely available — responding quickly, keeping customers in the loop, and not disappearing once the job is booked.

You won't find 24/7 on the name because that would be over-promising. But 23-7? That's close enough to mean it.

Every job is quoted honestly, with no line items that don't need to be there. The work is done to a standard Robert would be happy with on his own home. And if something isn't right, it gets fixed — no arguments, no extra invoices.
