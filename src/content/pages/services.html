What We Build

From boundary fences to backyard decks and full roof replacements, 23-7 Builders handles the jobs that matter most to Auckland homeowners. All work is carried out by Robert and his team — no subcontracting surprises, just straightforward trade work done properly.

  • Fencing — Timber, Aluminium & Steel

    A good fence does more than mark a boundary. It adds privacy, security, and kerb appeal — and in Auckland, it needs to stand up to everything the weather throws at it. We install timber, aluminium, and steel fencing for residential and commercial properties across Central, East, and South Auckland. Whether you need a new boundary fence, a replacement after a storm, or a pool fence that meets NZ Building Code clause F9 compliance, we'll measure up, quote clearly, and get it built to last.

  • Decks & Outdoor Living — Designed for Auckland

    A well-built deck is one of the best investments you can make in an Auckland home. It extends your living space, suits the climate, and adds real value when it comes time to sell. We work with both treated timber and low-maintenance composite decking, depending on your budget and how you want to use the space. All decks are designed and built to comply with the NZ Building Act — we'll handle building consent where it's required so you don't have to. From a simple ground-level platform to a multi-level entertainer's deck, we'll design something that works for your section and your lifestyle.

  • Roofing — Installs, Re-Roofing & Repairs

    Your roof is the most important part of your home and one of the most overlooked — until something goes wrong. We handle new roof installations, full re-roofing, and repairs across Auckland's residential and light commercial properties. We work with the most common NZ roofing materials — long-run steel (Colorsteel), concrete and clay tiles, and butyl membrane for flat or low-pitch roofs. Auckland's high rainfall and coastal salt air mean your roof takes a hammering year-round. We'll assess what you've got, give you an honest recommendation, and get it weather-tight. We also handle guttering and downpipe replacements as part of any roofing project.
